Here's an example email to the Head of Department (HOD) requesting weekend leave:

Subject: Request for Weekend Leave

Dear [HOD's Name],

I am writing to request a weekend leave for [Date, e.g., Saturday and Sunday]. I would like to take this opportunity to attend to some personal matters that require my attention during this time.

I have reviewed my workload and made arrangements to ensure that my tasks are completed before my leave. My colleagues will be able to manage my responsibilities while I am away.

If approved, I will ensure that all my tasks are up to date and completed before my leave. If there are any concerns or issues, please let me know and I will be happy to discuss alternative arrangements.

Thank you for considering my request. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Best regards,

[Your Name]

[Your Department]
[Your Position]

Note: You may need to customize the email based on your organization's policies and procedures.
